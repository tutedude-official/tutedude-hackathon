Problem statement

Preview (what students see)

Send it on WhatsApp (WATI)

Sends the problem statement on WhatsApp to everyone in a team of 2 or more, with the same Utility template as the reminders. It goes out by itself at 7:16 PM on 21 Nov if the problem statement is saved by then, and it can't go out before 7:15 PM. WhatsApp allows one paragraph of about 600 characters, so a longer statement is cut and the message says to read all of it on the team page.

Send a notification

It shows on the site and, for people who turned on notifications, on their phone.

WhatsApp reminder (WATI)

Sends a WhatsApp message to everyone who is alone in their team: get your teammates in before teams lock, or your team is out of the Buildathon. It goes out by itself once a day at 1 PM until teams lock. This button sends it right now. Each person gets a different message, picked at random.

Teams of one → teams of 5

Puts everyone who is alone in their team together, in teams of up to 5 (as even as possible, never a team of 1). In each group the oldest team stays, with its name, link and leader; the others join it. Everyone gets a notification on the site and phone, and a WhatsApp message with their new team and their teammates' names and WhatsApp numbers, so they can message each other. People not in any team are not touched.

    Submissions

    Leaders submit once from their team page, after the problem statement and before the deadline. “Reopen” removes a submission so that leader can submit again.
