Why a Buildathon?

Because learning alone is hard.

  • Meet learners beyond your course.
  • Learn from each other: everyone brings something different.
  • Study together on TuteDude Sessions.
  • Build proof: a real product and a certificate.

The prize is the bonus. The real win is your team.

What can we win?

₹5,000, ₹3,000 and ₹2,000 for the top 3 teams. Everyone gets a certificate.

Who can take part?

Every TuteDude student who has an idea and wants to build something that solves a real-world problem. Any course, any skill, no fixed roles. We check each email with TuteDude. It's free.

How do we register as a team?

The leader taps “Register your team”, adds their details and each teammate's name and email, and watches a 1-minute video. Done.

Do my teammates need to register too?

Yes, and it takes 1 minute. The leader shares the team link, each teammate opens it and joins the team with their own name, WhatsApp number and TuteDude email.

I don't have a team.

Come to a Team Building Session, every morning and evening on TuteDude Sessions, form a team, then register. Or join the Buildathon WhatsApp community, where students without a team find each other: Join the WhatsApp community ↗

We registered our team. What now?

Nothing to build yet. On 21 Nov, 7:15 PM the problem statement appears on your team page, with what to build. Then you have 1 week to build. Anyone in the team can submit the project on the team page as soon as it's ready, any time until Sat, 28 Nov, 11:59 PM.

What will we build?

On 21 Nov, 7:15 PM a real problem appears on your team page. Build a novel solution: a working product, landing page, brand kit, launch film and explainer video, plus posts on your project's Instagram and X.

Can I switch teams?

Yes. On your team page, tap Leave team (or Remove next to your name) and paste the team link of your new team, or just open that team's link. You move in one step, so you stay in the Buildathon. The new team needs room, and a team that has submitted its project is final.

Who can see my WhatsApp number?

Only your teammates and the organisers. Before you message anyone on WhatsApp you agree to the WhatsApp rules: only about the Buildathon, no spam, no unwanted messages. If someone troubles you, tell the organisers on TuteDude Community and we take action.
